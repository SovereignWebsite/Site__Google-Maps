google-maps.sovereignwebsite.site

Directions, and the cameras on the way

Type two addresses. Google Maps gives the route, and this page lists the public traffic cameras within 150 metres of it, in the order you will pass them. Press Play on one to see it, from its operator.

Not switched on yet. This site uses Google Maps on its owner's own key and budget, and the owner has not set the key yet. Everything else is here, in the open.

When you press the button, this page loads Google Maps and sends Google the two addresses. Nothing is sent to anyone before you press it, and this site keeps nothing.

Cameras on the way

    Why this is a sovereign website

    It uses a paid service from someone else, and still belongs to its owner. The key, the budget and the daily limit are the owner's own, in the owner's Google account; nothing runs on our servers. The site is plain files in a repository the owner controls: change the key, or swap Google for another map, and the rest stays.

    The cameras come from the open camera file of live.promptugal.news, metadata only, under CC BY 4.0 from PrompTugal.news. This site keeps a copy of the cameras with their operator's own position (889 of 913, most of them Transport for London's); the 24 whose position is only a town's centre are left out. A camera's picture is never stored here: it comes from its operator when you press Play, under the operator's terms, shown with each camera.
